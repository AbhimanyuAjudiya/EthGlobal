## 0. One-paragraph thesis

In 2026 the small crypto teams that clear $10k/week are almost all **fee-on-flow products bolted onto a venue that already has liquidity** — Hyperliquid builder codes, Polymarket builder codes, Solana memecoin terminals and Telegram bots — or **stablecoin apps that monetise interchange, FX spread and yield in a corridor where banks are slow**. Infrastructure (agent registries, data layers, privacy primitives, intents) wins hackathon prizes and grants but almost never produces operator revenue inside 12 months; where it does (CoW solvers, Fusion resolvers) it is concentrated in 1–3 teams. India adds a hard constraint: prediction markets and real-money gaming are banned, the RBI is actively hostile to stablecoins (new card crackdown reported *today*), and the 30% / 1% TDS regime is unchanged — so Indian *users* are a poor first market, while Indian *compliance pain* and Indian *export/freelance payouts* are real paid problems.

## 3. India & Mumbai angle

### 3.1 Tax: unchanged, now with teeth
- Budget 2026 (1 Feb) kept **30% on VDA gains, no loss offset, 1% TDS (Section 194S)**; added penalties effective 1 Apr 2026: **₹200/day** for late reporting and **₹50,000 (~$545)** for inaccurate disclosures.
