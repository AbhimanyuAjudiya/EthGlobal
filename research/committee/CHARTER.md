# Ideation Committee — Charter & Process

**Goal:** pick ONE project for ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026 (6–8 Nov; hacking opens 5 Nov 21:00 IST) that (a) can place top-10 finalist, (b) stacks 3 partner prizes with load-bearing integrations, and (c) has a credible path to ≥ US$10,000/week revenue post-hackathon.

**Inputs every member must read:** `research/sponsors.md`, `research/winners.md`, `research/ideation.md`, `research/data/*` (finalist + sponsor-track winner history, 2024–2026). Prior-art check against the full showcase CSV is mandatory for every proposal.

## Members (10)
| # | Role | Lens | Round |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Judge's Eye** | Top-10 finalist under the 5-category rubric (Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability, WOW) | Proposer (R0), Rebuttal (R2) |

| 3 | **Sponsor Stacker** | Max EV from 3 partner programs; new-sponsor soft competition; submission hygiene | Proposer (R0), Rebuttal (R2) |

| 10 | **Devil's Advocate / Synthesizer** | Tries to kill the top 3; then writes the decision memo answering every open question | R2 + R3 |

## Rounds
- **R0 Proposals** — members 1–4 each submit 3 ideas using the template below → `round0-<role>.md`.
- **R1 Cross-examination** — members 5–9 read all 12 proposals, score each 1–10 on their lens with written reasoning, and raise numbered questions → `round1-<role>.md`.
- **R2 Rebuttal & Debate** — proposers answer every numbered question against their ideas (or concede); Devil's Advocate attacks the top 3 by aggregate score → `round2-*.md`.
- **R3 Decision** — Synthesizer produces `DECISION.md`: the chosen idea, the runner-up, a table of *every* question raised and its resolution, sponsor map, revenue model, scope for the hackathon window, and explicit go/no-go risks. Chair (orchestrator) ratifies.

## Proposal template (mandatory fields)
1. Name + one-liner
2. User & pain (specific persona, specific moment)
3. Why now (Nov 2026 narrative + sponsor push)
4. Core mechanism (what is actually novel; what runs onchain vs off)
5. Sponsor map: 3 primary partner programs + exact tracks; for each, "what breaks if this sponsor's product is removed?"
6. Revenue model with arithmetic to $10k/week (fee %, volume, users, price) and the first 3 paying customers
7. Hackathon-window scope: what is live and demoable in the 4-minute pitch; what is faked/mocked (be honest)
8. Prior art: grep of showcase CSV + web; what is different
9. Biggest risk & mitigation
10. UI/UX reference companies (best-in-class in this category)
11. Finalist rubric self-score (T/O/P/U/W out of 10 each) with one-line justification each

## Open strategic question for all members
**From-scratch vs Continuity.** From-scratch work may only begin after kickoff (5 Nov 21:00 IST). Building the open-source base *before* the event and entering **Continuity** (judged on progress during the window) unlocks continuity-only prizes (1inch top tier, World [Cont], Uniswap $5k, 0G $3k, Arkiv $4k, Arc $3k, Whitechain $3k, Hedera, Definitive $1.5k) with less competition, but finalist judging weighs from-scratch wow. Each member must state a position.
