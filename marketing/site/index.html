Questions

The source is on GitHub.

Who pays for the free 300 MB?

The event or brand that funds the campaign, in USDC.

Which phones take the eSIM?

Any eSIM-capable phone that is not locked to a carrier.

Can I claim the free eSIM twice?

No. It is one free bundle per human, not per phone.

What does an eSIM top-up cost?

1 GB for 7 days is $4.00, paid in USDC.

Where are my eSIM receipts?

Receipts list every payment with its on-chain hash.
