# Round 1 — Crypto-Native Power User (member #8)

**Lens.** ~200 crypto apps installed and deleted; I live in Hyperliquid, Polymarket and Telegram bots. One test: would a real person (Devcon foreigner, Indian hacker, chaiwala, signals follower) open this twice unpaid, and does it look designed or like the 4,000th purple-gradient dashboard. Tap counts are first-time, on a phone.

**Three facts that reshape every journey.**
1. **World ID in India is Selfie Check or NFC-passport "Official ID"; Orb has been paused there since Dec 2023.** Nine of twelve ideas put a World App install (~100 MB, phone number, selfie) before first value for Indians, while most Devcon foreigners already hold Orb IDs.
2. **Arc mainnet is seven weeks old.** Nobody landing at BOM holds USDC on Arc; they hold it on Base, Arbitrum or Solana. Every "pay on Arc" flow hides a 5–8 tap bridge before the first tap of value.
3. **Uniswap's own web app has had an Auctions (CCA) tab since 2 Feb 2026** and self-serve creation since June; Aztec's CCA drew 17k bidders.

## The twelve

### 1. Dhwani — stablecoin soundbox
**Journey.** Merchant: 4 taps (plug in, scan, name). Payer online: open wallet, scan the OLED QR, confirm: 3 taps *if* the wallet knows chain 5042 and holds Arc USDC; otherwise "unknown network", a bridge, 12+ taps. Offline: install PWA, fund an embedded key ("a second wallet?"), airplane mode, hold the phone to the box through a 6–12 s chirp; Tez's near-instant Cash Mode never became a habit.
**Twice?** Merchant: yes, structurally; a soundbox needs zero attention and the voice is the receipt. Payer: yes if the box takes the USDC I hold; no if Arc-only.
**Share.** Video: chai stall, box announcing "paanch sau rupaye prapt hue" after a foreigner's phone chirps. Virality 9/10.
**References.** Right for hardware, blind to software. Best: Paytm Soundbox 4.0, Square, Stripe Terminal, Toast, Teenage Engineering/Nothing. **Signature:** one recorded human voice, never robotic TTS; a thermal-strip monospace receipt; an OLED showing only the amount.
**Desire 8/10.**
- Q-USER-DHWANI-1: Multi-chain EIP-681 (Base/Arbitrum/Polygon USDC) or Arc only? If Arc-only, show the bridge path and tap count on a judge's phone.
- Q-USER-DHWANI-2: Is the offline PWA a second wallet or a session key from the main wallet (7702/4337)? How is it funded?
- Q-USER-DHWANI-3: Measured chirp duration and decode rate at ~70 dB ambient?

### 2. Boli — Indic speech sold to agents
**Journey.** Seller: WhatsApp link, PWA, Play Store, World App, phone number, Selfie Check (no Orb in India), back to Boli, hold-to-record, 0G grading wait, "₹2.50 earned" in Hedera USDC. 10–12 taps, five-plus minutes, four confusions.
**Twice?** Seller: only if payout lands in UPI or a balance redeemable for mobile recharge; USDC she cannot spend is not pay.
**Share.** The "₹ earned by this room" leaderboard; hackers screenshot it, sellers never will. Virality 5/10.
**References.** Best: Karya, Toloka, Prolific, Scale Rapid, Duolingo speaking exercise. **Signature:** vernacular-first: one big hold-to-speak circle, the prompt in a real Devanagari face (Mukta/Baloo), the grade stub shown *before* the money.
**Desire 5/10.**
- Q-USER-BOLI-1: How does Sunita turn Hedera USDC into rupees: which off-ramp, who eats the 1% TDS and spread, how many days?
- Q-USER-BOLI-2: At Selfie level, does one-human-one-seller hold, and does World App install survive a ₹8,000 Android with 2 GB free?

### 3. Bhishi — auction chit fund
**Journey.** WhatsApp link, connect wallet (withdraw from CoinDCX, bridge to Arc), World App, approve, join (bond plus contribution), wait a month. 15+ taps. Monthly: approve, contribute, bid, with 1% TDS per transfer and 30% on the "dividend", which is netted invisibly, so the best moment is the one you cannot see.
**Twice?** Monthly by construction if the organiser drags me in; I would never seek it. The tax surface alone kills a ₹5,000 chit for a salaried Indian.
**Share.** The Bhishi Rate ticker. Virality 4/10.
**References.** Best: Splitwise, Monzo/Revolut group pots, Kalshi (clearing display), KSFE Chitty, Zerodha Kite. **Signature:** the khata: ruled paper ledger, a monthly rubber stamp, the auction as a countdown dial; no charts.
**Desire 3/10.**
- Q-USER-BHISHI-1: What does a member *see* on dividend day? If netted silently, how does "you earned ₹412" become a moment?

### 4. CallSheet — sealed executable calls in Telegram
**Journey.** /start inside Telegram, vault (generated wallet, or WalletConnect, which breaks on iOS?), deposit USDC on Unichain (nobody holds USDC there), follow a caller, sealed card, reveal, batch fill, exits via Definitive on Base: a second chain. ~10 taps, two chains, two balances. Trojan onboards in 3 taps by generating the wallet; copy that.
**Twice?** Yes: the only idea built for people who already do this daily, and sealing fixes a pain I have paid for (front-run by the caller). But callers call HL perps and Solana memes, not Unichain spot; a uniform-price batch into a $20k pool is a slippage machine. The product wants to be an HL builder-code front end; the prize pulls it off its venue.
**Share.** The reveal card "CALL #41 · +12.4% · 83 followers filled at 1.2013" is the pvp.trade/Hyperliquid PnL-card genre. Virality 8/10.
**References.** Trojan's inline keyboards, not GMGN density, fit Telegram. Best: pvp.trade, Trojan/BONKbot, Hyperliquid (PnL card), PolyCop, Axiom. **Signature:** the sealed envelope: the call arrives redacted with black bars and a countdown, un-redacts at reveal; every fill receipt shows three lines, you / caller / protocol. Monospace numerals, zero gradients.
**Desire 7/10.**
- Q-USER-CALLSHEET-1: Where does follower USDC live; can the whole loop run on Base mainnet with $5 fills instead of Unichain Sepolia plus Base; and is the wallet generated (3 taps) or WalletConnect-in-Telegram (10 taps, iOS-hostile)?
- Q-USER-CALLSHEET-2: What does a demo call trade on Unichain and how deep is that pool?

### 5. Nilami — one-human-one-bid CCA launchpad
**Journey.** Launch page, World App, connect, bridge to Unichain, commit (tx 1), return for reveal (tx 2; miss it, lose it), claim (tx 3), dividend claims (tx 4+). Six transactions for one allocation; Uniswap's Auctions tab does it in two taps, ungated.
**Twice?** Launchpad users return when launches moon, not when they are fair; people who want fairness do not trade memes. The bidder dividend is the one clever hook.
**Share.** Live clearing on the big screen: "3,120 humans, one price: $0.042". Virality 6/10.
**References.** Clanker/Flaunch/Zora/pump.fun are the bot-casino aesthetic Nilami opposes. Best: Uniswap Auctions tab (the incumbent), Echo/Sonar, Legion, Coinlist, Fjord Foundry. **Signature:** the auction house: a clearing line that moves only at reveal, bids as human silhouettes stacking, an allocation receipt like a share certificate; serif numerals.
**Desire 4/10.**
- Q-USER-NILAMI-1: Why would a bidder leave the Uniswap web app's Auctions tab for a World App install plus commit-reveal?
- Q-USER-NILAMI-2: What happens to the bidder who commits and misses the reveal window?

### 9. Kilowatt — telemetry-metered battery leases
**Journey.** Driver never sees a wallet (paymaster), good, until "battery locked, pay ₹X" because an ML score said so. Investor: bridge to Arc, deposit into a waterfall vault, ~6 taps.

### 10. Recharge — eSIM data by USDC, gifted by ENS, topped up by agent
**Journey.** Lena at BOM on airport Wi-Fi: poster QR, page, World ID (Orb-verified already: 2 taps), "300 MB free, install", Apple's eSIM sheet via universal link (3 taps; she cannot scan a QR on the same phone), Data Roaming on, data flows. 7–8 taps, one unavoidable confusion. Top-up must take USDC from Base, not Hedera, or it is a bridge again. Agent: "allow up to $10 of top-ups" needs honest framing.
**Twice?** Yes: the only idea I would use on day 1 and day 4 unprompted. The pain is real (3DS failing on airport Wi-Fi, Jio counter KYC). Sending `rahul.eth` 1 GB at 3 a.m. is a crypto-native primitive that is genuinely delightful.
**Share.** "I got 300 MB in Mumbai for being human", plus gift notifications ("vitalik.eth sent you 1 GB"). Virality 7/10.
**References.** Best: Saily (cleanest install), Airalo (plan cards), Revolut (in-app eSIM), Apple's eSIM sheet, Jio/Paytm recharge screen. **Signature:** the recharge voucher: one big MB meter styled as a prepaid scratch card with a "USDC per 100 MB" ticker; gifts as boarding-pass cards addressed to an ENS name. The orange FreeCharge era, not glass.
**Desire 8/10.**

### 11. Challan — TDS-withholding v4 hook
**Journey.** Arjun must find a Challan-routed swap UI (the hook lives on specific pools), connect, World ID plus a residency credential that mostly does not exist (Orb paused; most Indian passports lack an NFC chip), or self-attest to pay 1% more, swap, "TDS 1% · ₹412 withheld", certificate. 8 taps plus a credential nobody has.
**Twice?** No Indian voluntarily routes through a pool that withholds 1% when the identical pool is one tab away. Compliance is bought by institutions (Okto, CoinSwitch) and avoided by users; this is a licence to wallet integrators, and the proposer's own U6 admits it is invisible when it works.
**Share.** Indian CT would argue about it: reach, not love; half the replies are "you built the IT department a hook". Virality 3/10.
**References.** Add Quicko. **Signature:** the challan itself: the government receipt format (CIN, BSR code, date) rendered beautifully, one extra line on the swap sheet, Zerodha-grade whitespace.
**Desire 3/10** (6 as a wallet licence).
- Q-USER-CHALLAN-1: Why would Arjun choose the withholding pool over the free identical pool; is the real user Arjun or Okto?
- Q-USER-CHALLAN-2: How does an Indian prove residency to World ID 4.0 today, and what does the self-attested fallback do to the word "compliant"?

### 12. Udhaar — a UPI tab with a verified local

**Twice?** Once, when stuck. The informal version ("bro pay, I'll send USDT") is faster because the settler is my friend beside me; the app adds stranger-matching plus accounting, and matching is the weak link. NPCI piloted UPI One World for delegates in Feb 2026 and plans to open it to all inbound tourists this winter: possibly this venue, this week.
**Share.** "A stranger paid my vada pav and I sent her USDC"; settlers earning 1.5% will share. Virality 6/10.
**References.** Right. Best: Splitwise (tab), Binance P2P (counterparty trust, timers), Wise (rate transparency), Cash App, GPay scan flow. **Signature:** the khata: a running tab styled as the shopkeeper's red-cloth ledger, each line merchant + ₹ + USDC, settled with a rubber stamp. A tab, not a wallet.
**Desire 5/10.**
- Q-USER-UDHAAR-1: p50 seconds from QR scan to the vendor's soundbox chirp with a stranger settler, and what happens when no settler is in range?

## Chair's cross-cutting questions

**(A) From-scratch vs Continuity.** From-scratch: users do not care which track we entered, and finalists discount continuity. But hackathon projects never ship onboarding, the one thing a head start buys. Spend the pre-window on UX that is not code: ten merchant and ten foreigner interviews, Hindi/Marathi copy, the recorded voice, eSIM inventory, a strict design brief (type, palette, one signature object). Check with ETHGlobal whether mock-ups count as project work; disclose either way.

**(B) 100 real users during Devcon week.** Recharge: a venue table plus one sponsor-funded welcome bundle gets 100 installs in a morning. Dhwani: ten boxes at F&B stalls reach 100 payers *only if* the QR accepts the chains people hold. CallSheet: 100 hacker-followers in one Telegram group with $5 real fills on Base mainnet. Asli and Boli can stunt to 100 Indian hackers for $10 or ₹2.50 if the payout is spendable. Udhaar: 100 one-time uses at the gate with 20 recruited settlers; Diwali Sunday shutters the stalls. The rest: no.

**(C) CT dunk list.** Octroi ("x402 wrapper four thousand, with a face scan"). Gavel ("a Dune dashboard with an agent button"). Asli ("Galxe but Worldcoin"). Nilami ("fair launch 127; the bots are already in the Discord"). Bhishi ("twenty-eighth chit fund, zero finalists"). Challan splits the timeline: respect for the hook, "bootlicker" from the rest. Kilowatt gets dunked as dystopian, not slop. Dhwani, Recharge, CallSheet and Udhaar cannot be called slop: a box that talks, data that works, a PnL card, a stranger buying your vada pav. The slop tell is visual: purple gradients, Inter, glass cards, emoji in headings. Each signature above is a physical or cultural object a model would not pick.
