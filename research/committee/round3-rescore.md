## 6. Verdict

No v2 satisfies both mandates; the re-score prices the gap. Dhwani v2 is the best prize play ($3.7k EV, 10% finalist, the only object on the table) and the worst business (base $1k/week, 6% at the bar): it fixed every technical critique and restated the commercial one. Lifafa is the mirror image — the strongest revenue instrument here (base $3.5k, 20% at the bar, a real supply-side wedge) with the weakest judge shape ($2.2k, 5%) and a false custody claim. Recharge × Octroi is never last on any lens: within $500 of Dhwani on EV, clears the Judge's 6% threshold, survives Security's CT audit if sold online only, has the highest P(ship) at 0.60, and alone has a buyer in the hall Thursday and cash Friday. By the critics' own thresholds it best satisfies BOTH mandates — provided it adopts SS's security design, settles Solidity vs No-Solidity, deletes stand sales and treats Thursday's sponsor meeting as the revenue test. The committee gives up Dhwani's finalist shape (~$460 of EV), Lifafa's 20% revenue chance halved to 10%, the Uniswap, Definitive, Arc and 0G pools, and a WOW bigger than a scratch card. If Thursday fails, Recharge is a $2k/week retail business and Lifafa, with the dedicated-wallet fix, is the revenue-first alternative.
