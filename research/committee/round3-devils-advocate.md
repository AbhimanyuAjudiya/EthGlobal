**Brief and method.** Kill each surviving v2 with arguments nobody has made; every attack names the text it contradicts and the test that falsifies it. Six searches, all on verdict-changing facts. (1) [ETHGlobal rules](https://ethglobal.com/rules): pre-existing project-specific work outside an approved Continuity track makes a project "ineligible for Partner prizes and the Finalist category"; Continuity needs documented pre-existing work, *only in-window work is judged*, and partner eligibility *varies by partner*; no minimum age for the "existing" project is stated anywhere I found ([Cannes](https://ethglobal.com/events/cannes2026/info/details)). (2) Hyperliquid's `approveBuilderFee` accepts `hyperliquidChain: "Testnet"` ([Chainstack](https://docs.chainstack.com/reference/hyperliquid-exchange-approve-builder-fee.md), [Privy](https://docs.privy.io/recipes/hyperliquid/builder-codes)). (3) HL `userFills` is public, no auth, any address, 2,000 most recent fills ([HL docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint.md)). (4) eSIM Access partner accounts are self-serve, "in minutes" ([eSIM Access](https://esimaccess.com/console-app/)); India wholesale is still unpublished. (5) No Hedera Mumbai listing is indexed; Hedera's ETHOnline 2026 x402 text reportedly required a live service, "not a prototype", and its continuity prize went to teams returning to something "already built on the network" ([Genfinity](https://genfinity.io/2026/08/27/hedera-15k-bounties-ethonline-2026-agentic-ai-payment-tokenization/)).

## 0. One attack that applies to all three: the continuity catch-22

Every v2 adopted the same structure — pre-build the core, ship *every sponsor integration* in the window — so the in-window delta is honest. Now read the sponsor continuity lanes: Hedera "move an existing **Hedera** project forward", 0G "**Keep Building** on 0G", Arkiv "continuity for **existing** projects", Arc "bring your project to Arc". A project with zero pre-window sponsor usage is the opposite of what three of those four lanes describe, and the from-scratch lanes are forfeited by the public pre-build. Each sponsor prize therefore rests on a discretionary call — whether that partner lets a Continuity entrant compete in its from-scratch tracks — that nobody in R2 priced per partner. The Sponsor Stacker's "zero eligibility risk" is wrong: the risk moved from ETHGlobal to each partner. Second, a repository created 3 Nov and entered as an "existing project" on 5 Nov is a 48-hour-old existing project; the letter allows it, the finalist judge's reading of the commit history is unknowable, and "projects that only repackage pre-event work won't place" is the sentence they will quote. Per-partner specifics are item (5) of each section.

## 1. Dhwani v2 (`round2-judges-eye.md` §2)

**(1) Three new arguments.**
*a. Multi-chain acceptance made Arc optional — in the proposer's own text.* To answer Q-USER-DHWANI-1, v2 relays "on any chain it holds gas for (demo: Arc with USDC gas, Base Sepolia with an ETH float)" and degrades "Arc RPC down → the Base path still speaks". The sponsor map's "remove Arc → offline mode impossible" is therefore false by the proposer's own scope: the relayer float belongs to Dhwani-the-company either way, so "a chaiwala cannot manage two assets" never applied to the relay. The async Arc judge reads the degradation table and sees one chain of three — the "decorative" standard this committee used to strip Arc from Recharge.
*b. The offline payer must be online first, in our app.* A chirp needs a PWA installed, a key funded (embedded today, 7702 later) and USDC on the right chain — all done with connectivity, in advance, specifically for Dhwani. The persona either has data (QR works; the chirp is a party trick) or has none and never installed the PWA (nothing works). The honest user is "my data ran out after I set this up this morning" — a population that already has a payment method. On stage the chirp will come from a team phone. The WOW is real; the Practicality behind it is not.
*c. The khata is a public revenue ledger for an informal merchant.* Every receipt is an Arkiv entity {merchant, chain, amount, INR rate, tx hash}, queryable by anyone — that is the point of "verifiable export". A stall merchant below the GST threshold now has an indexed daily-revenue feed the tax department, a landlord or a rival can query. Security made this argument for Challan's traders; nobody made it for Dhwani's merchant, and v2 put `expiresIn` on the *payer* pseudonym only. Encrypting payloads fixes it and weakens the "third-party verifiable khata" that justifies Arkiv.

**(2) Weakest revenue claim.** "Two events/month at $120k vendor GMV" through the box at 1.5%. Conference F&B is organiser-catered or paid in local fiat; vendor-side *stablecoin* GMV at a 10k-person event is plausibly $5–20k. At $15k/event the line is ≈ $105/week, not $1.3k, and base falls from $3.3k to ≈ $2.1k/week. The remaining $2k is the conceded hinge (one PSP shipping 2,000 boxes); hinge false too → ≈ $100/week, the proposer's own low. P(base) = P(PSP signs by Q1) × P(events at that GMV); the first is a coin flip in the proposer's words, the second never got a number.

**(3) Single point of failure in the demo.** Audio on hardware that does not exist yet. The Pi and ReSpeaker/USB audio must be bought Wed 4 Nov (Dhanteras Friday and Diwali Sunday kill couriers), and someone must have shipped ALSA/I2S before. The cutover rule covers a failed chirp, not a box that cannot speak. Plan C ("a laptop as the box") takes W10 to W5 and removes the physical object that triples finalist odds — the whole 12% sits on one Wednesday courier. A decode rate under 80% demotes the chirp to video, and the image becomes a QR payment with a recording: Web3 Soundbox (Paris 2023) in better plastic.

**(4) Sponsor-judge objections.** *Arc:* "The judge's payment settles on Base; your own fallback runs the offline relay with an ETH float. Where does USDC-as-gas change the product?" — denies the $3k DeFi-app track; "bring your project to Arc" survives at lower odds. *Arkiv:* "One box, receipts with a TTL, and the only load-bearing read is a demo question — Postgres with `expiresIn`; the multi-box aggregation the Veteran Judge asked for is out of scope (single box is a declared mock)." *0G:* "An LLM mapping three fixed Marathi questions to three queries is not meaningful integration (0G repo-reviews 100% of submissions), and 'Agents with a Body' is for agents that act through a body — yours plays a recording." Expect Keep Building/Wildcard money at best, not the $5k.

**(5) Continuity risk.** 0G is the sharp edge: the claimed track ("Agents with a Body", $5k) is from-scratch; 0G's continuity lane is "Keep Building on 0G" ($3k), meaningless for a project with no prior 0G, and prizes are "at 0G's discretion". If 0G confines Continuity entrants to Keep Building, the third sponsor is worth ≤$3k and the ENS fallback has an unpublished breakdown. Arc's "bring your project to Arc" is the best-fitting continuity lane of any v2 — the one sponsor whose load-bearing claim I just removed.

**(6) The quote-tweet.** "They built a Raspberry Pi that says 'five hundred rupees' in Marathi when a Devcon tourist pays a chaiwala in a stablecoin he can't legally hold, on a seven-week-old chain nobody holds it on, with an LLM so he can ask how much he didn't make. The chaiwala was a teammate."

## 3. Lifafa (`round2-operator-revenue.md` §2)

**(1) Three new arguments.**
*a. The skin-in-game rule leaks the sealed call.* The caller's own fill must land within 60 s of the commit, verified from the public fills API — and `userFills` takes any address with no auth (verified). To verify, Lifafa must know the caller's HL address; for "venue-verified PnL" and "the caller's position stays visible on HL", that address is public. So every watcher sees asset, side, size and entry within seconds of the commit — free, before T, before any unseal is bought; what stays sealed is stop and target, worth far less than the entry. Hide the address until T and (i) "venue-verified" becomes "trust Lifafa's server", and (ii) after the first reveal the address is burned for every later call — rotating sub-accounts is linkable through the master's funding transfers. Sealing is theatre by construction, and what makes it theatre is the proposer's own anti-insider fix.
*b. The deep-book defence deletes the follower's pain.* Pre-positioning was answered by restricting calls to BTC/ETH/SOL/HYPE books where "300 followers' flow does not move price". In those books a 40-second delay costs single-digit bps; the R0 pain ("sees the call 40 s late at a worse price") does not exist on the venue chosen to make the caller harmless. Atomic reveal and one-price fills solve a thin-book problem on a venue that now forbids thin books. What remains is a paid signal group with HL execution and a leaderboard — pvp.trade with a paywall; O6 is O4.
*c. The fee is on execution, the paywall is on information, and they decouple at T.* Builder fees accrue only to orders carrying Lifafa's builder param. A follower who paid $1 to unseal can trade the revealed call on app.hyperliquid.xyz at 0 bps; an agent — the Hedera story — will *always* route its own order, because code-enforced execution is its whole point. Agent revenue is therefore 20% of $0.50–5 unseals, and human builder-fee capture is a fraction of volume. Add VARA: the first payers are Dubai HL-perps groups, and VARA's rulebook lists Advisory Services among its seven licensed VA activities, with marketing rules it enforces; paying Dubai callers half a fee to publish paid trading calls moves the exposure from FIU-IND (answered) to VARA (never raised). In Lifafa's favour: testnet builder codes work, so the demo risk the proposer flagged is gone.

**(2) Weakest revenue claim.** 100% builder-code capture. Base = 300 actives × $8k/day × 4 bps = $960/day assumes every mirrored fill carries the code. At 40% capture: ≈ $2.7k + $0.3k unseals + $0.5k subs ≈ $3.5k/week. If the 300 actives (three LOIs that do not exist on 3 Nov) are 100: ≈ $1.2k/week. Both false: the low case ($0.9k) with Dexari's decay curve ($129k → $8k/month inside a year) on top.

**(3) Single point of failure.** The envelope. "At 0:00 a real Diwali envelope with the commitment hash printed on it is handed to a judge … the caller seals a BTC call … the judge tears the paper and the call matches." For a pre-printed hash to match a call sealed live, the call was fixed when the envelope was printed — hours earlier. A sharp judge: "So the call was decided this morning, and you had six hours to position before your 'skin-in-game' fill." The physical device dramatises the insider problem, in front of the judge, with the proposer's hands. Fix: print the hash live on a thermal printer — then the trust is in the laptop, and the moment is a receipt, not a lifafa.

**(4) Sponsor-judge objections.** *Hedera x402:* "An agent that pays to unseal a trading call and mirrors it is a copy-bot with a paywall; the track wants data APIs, and the buyer agent is yours — plus you are in Continuity." *World:* "You accept Device/Document and label it; at $0.50 an account the 'one human, one caller record' that launder-proofs the leaderboard is a price. The 5-vs-10 bps discount changes terms; the record integrity does not." *Arkiv:* "HCS already timestamps commitments; Arkiv mirrors HCS plus your indexer. Which read is load-bearing — the leaderboard? That is a dashboard."

**(5) Continuity risk.** Worse than Recharge's. Lifafa's pre-build is HL integration, commit-reveal and the bot; Hyperliquid is not a sponsor, so under Continuity the "existing project" is 100% non-sponsor code and the in-window delta is sponsor glue — the bolt-on pattern `winners.md` §4.5 names, legible straight off the commit history. Hedera continuity ("existing Hedera project") does not describe it; x402 is from-scratch; World [Cont] wants a trust decision "e.g. gating", and Lifafa gates at Device level. The only lane that clearly fits is Arkiv.

**(6) The quote-tweet.** "Sealed alpha where the caller's fill is public on Hyperliquid sixty seconds after sealing. You can copy him on Hypurrscan for free, but they'd like 10 bps and ₹40 to open the envelope. The envelope was printed this morning. Happy Diwali."

## 4. The under-weighted third option

**Octroi standalone is not under-weighted; it is correctly weighted and getting worse.** Its biggest line (Hedera x402, $6.5k) is from-scratch, so Continuity cuts its sponsor EV from ≈$2.75k to ≈$1.8k; VC 2/10, W4 and the Power User's "face scan for a weather API" all stand. Dismiss.

**Asli, re-cut as action-conditioned data drops, is the option the committee's own evidence points at and nobody proposed.** Three members voted it third; the Operator parked it because "a dashboard row turns green". But: (i) the buyer Recharge needs — sponsor UA budget — *is* Asli's buyer, and my badge argument kills "one bundle per human" without touching "one bundle per verified human *who deployed on your testnet*": an action a badge cannot verify and the exact thing devrel budgets pay for (13 partners already pay $15k pools to reach builders). (ii) Asli's two fatal problems vanish when the payout is data: no USDC to Indian micro-earners (FIU/TDS posture, the Power User's cash-out), and the image is a phone lighting up, not a row. (iii) Ops: 65 ph, P(ship) 0.60 — highest in the deck; Security 5/10 as "cheaper fraud" with tiered pricing; VC: P(≥$1 in 30 days) 40%, the highest, because the sales meeting is the hackathon. (iv) The strongest continuity story available: an escrow-plus-verifier platform whose in-window delta is onboarding *real sponsor campaigns* — the sponsors judging it are its first customers.
Honest numbers by the VC's method: low $0–200/week; base $0.5–1.5k ($2.5–7.5k campaign spend/week at 20%); high $4–6k if two chains adopt it as a standing rail; P(≥$10k/week by month 9) ≈ 5% — the VC's "two $25k campaigns every week" objection stands. Prize EV ≈ $1.4k plus a 3% finalist shot, the lowest of the four. Asli v2 is the lowest-variance, lowest-ceiling choice: it maximises P(first dollar) and P(ship), minimises legal exposure, and gives up the finalist slot and the $10k ceiling. If the founder's revenue mandate means "a paying customer in 30 days", it wins; if it means "the ceiling", it loses to all three.

## 5. Verdict

**Dhwani v2 survives best.** My attacks on it cost sponsor probability (Arc optional, 0G thin, Arkiv privacy) and expose offline mode as a prepared-user feature; none touches what carries it — a physical object a judge's phone makes talk, revenue disclosed honestly, no custody, no sale on Indian soil, and the two best-fitting continuity lanes (Arc "bring your project", Arkiv existing-project). My attacks on Recharge remove its base-case revenue and its legal fix in one move; my attacks on Lifafa break the mechanism, the pain and the instrument's capture. The committee must say aloud what this choice means: Dhwani fails the $10k mandate most visibly — base ≈ $2k/week after my haircut, ≈ $100 without the PSP — and a 12% finalist shot is being allowed to stand in for a business. Put that sentence in `DECISION.md`.

**I reverse under two conditions.**
1. **Hardware gate, Wed 4 Nov 23:59 IST.** Pi (or Pi 4) with working audio in hand, the box speaking a composed amount from the voice bank, and ≥80% chirp decode at 20 cm / 70 dB over 20 trials. Fail either, and Dhwani is a QR payment with a recording (W5); I reverse to **Recharge × Octroi with the stand removed** — free tier plus online paid lane only — because it needs no hardware, has desire 8 and takes cash.
2. **Revenue-evidence gate, Thu 5 Nov 21:00 IST.** If Lifafa holds one signed LOI from a ≥3k-member HL-perps group *and* the mechanism is rewritten to survive 3(1)(a) — commit from a fresh sub-account, reveal the address only at T, "venue-verified" downgraded to "Lifafa-verified until T", envelope printed live — while Dhwani holds zero PSP or organiser LOIs, I reverse to **Lifafa**: one idea would then have evidence for the revenue mandate and the others none, and its demo SPOF (testnet builder codes) is resolved. Without the LOI, Lifafa is a prize play with a 5% finalist shot and the Security critic's quote-tweet already written.

If ETHGlobal staff say at check-in that a repo created on 3 Nov is not an eligible Continuity base, archive the pre-build private and enter from-scratch with human-authored code. That lowers all three EVs equally and changes none of the above.
