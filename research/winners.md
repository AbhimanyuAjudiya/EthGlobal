# What Wins at ETHGlobal — Finalists & Sponsor-Track Winners (2024 → 2026)

**Purpose:** evidence base for the Mumbai 2026 ideation committee (see `research/committee/CHARTER.md`): pick an idea that can (a) make the top-10, (b) stack 3 load-bearing partner prizes. Compiled 2026-11-02. Companion: `research/sponsors.md` (Mumbai tracks/rules) and `research/data/*` (raw corpus).

**Sources & confidence.** `ethglobal.com` and most sponsor blogs are blocked in this session, so web material is from search snippets of ETHGlobal's X announcements, prize pages and sponsor recaps (0G, Arc, The Graph, Uniswap Foundation, 1inch, Blockscout, Flow, Zircuit, Mopro, Fern, Inco, SQD) and media round-ups (The Block, TechFlow, PANews, Bitget, crypto.news). The decisive source is the scraped showcase corpus in `research/data/`: `finalists-2024-2026.txt` (16 events, 149 finalists with every prize badge), `corpus-ethglobal-{new-york,cannes,lisbon}-2026-winners.md`, `corpus-ethonline-2026-winners.md`, `sponsor-track-winners-2025-2026.txt` (Uniswap 53 rows, Chainlink 34, World 163, 1inch 108, ENS 243, Hedera 71, 0G 39, Arc 47, EF 14; Arkiv/Whitechain/Definitive/Shutter 0), `ny2026-finalist-winnings.md`. Where the corpus and a snippet disagree, the corpus wins. Every inference is marked **(inferred)**.

**Rules that frame everything.** Standard rubric, identical on every event page incl. Mumbai: **Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability (UI/UX/DX), WOW Factor** (https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details). Finalist judging = live **4-min demo + 3-min Q&A**; partner prizes are judged **asynchronously** from the written submission + video; async events screen the top ~20% into live judging. Teams select **up to 3 partner prize programs**; several tracks from one partner count as **one** program (https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2025/info/details). Classic track code must start at kickoff; Continuity tracks are judged on progress in the window and unlock continuity-only prizes (details in `sponsors.md`).

## Part 4 — ANALYSIS

### 4.1 Categories that recur among finalists (149 finalists, 2024 → 2026)
1. **Agent economy — payments, identity, security, verification.** Share of finalists ≈20% in 2024–25 in-person events (DAOGenie, Industry AI, HelloACAI, Autonome, payload.exchange, Paybot, conduct.chat, MCPay…) → **≈44% of the 48 finalists in 2026** (Immunity, Wallet Shift, Accrue, Glassbox402, Hands Unchained, ethui, ENShell, DIVE, maki, ALMA, Corpus, npmguard, OpenBook, Novi Corpus, Cordon, OnchainRouter, Petri, claw2claw, BorrowBot, Oikonomos, router402). The 2026 sub-theme is **agents that pay (x402/Nanopayments), are constrained (ENShell, maki, Cordon, Aegis) or are verified (DIVE, Immunity, Wallet Shift/ERC-8004)** — not chat UIs.
2. **Prediction markets & parametric risk** — 3 of NYC 2026's 10 (LYNX, Cumulant, Canary) plus DIVE, Blip Market, PulsePlay, Dike, JetLagged, PolyBet, livestakes, Signals, PvPvAI, TX Delay Insurance: ≈13 finalists, all since 2025.
3. **Privacy / ZK / FHE / post-quantum** — ≈20 finalists (Nox, zkx402, LensMint, OnlyDanks, POAPrivacy, PrivyCycle, PrivacyLinks, Pace Club, DUST.OPS, Wrld Map, HowDoxxedAmI?, fheProxies, Zubernetes, Cat In A Box, Lortnoc Tahc, EVM PORST, Lean Silicon, VEIL VPN, GrimSwap, EthVaultPQ). Cannes 2025 was half privacy.
4. **Real-world payments with a local or physical hook** — ETHPark-QR, Angpao, cellfi (SMS), NoNet (offline), Solva, BeamPay, Primer (Amazon), Swap Pay, calendefi, Halo, Distro, explorador, Text-to-Chain, ETHastic (LoRa).
5. **DeFi mechanism design, mostly Uniswap v4 / 1inch Aqua** — ArcBook, Yoga, Aqua0, UniPerp, Detox-Hook, 0xCollateral, Hashlocked, UniV4 Backtester, Marina Royale, ALMA, TARE, Cumulant, LYNX.
6. **Physical / hardware demos** — ≈15% of 2026 finalists: Hands Unchained (robot arm), Lean Silicon (chip), Carte Bleue (card reader), PaintGlobal (NFC), maki (Pi), ETH Arcade, LeekWallet, plus 2024's OmiSwap wearable and LensMint camera. Pure games fell from 4/10 (Bangkok 2024) to ≤1 per 2026 event.
7. **Developer/security tooling** — reliably 1–2 slots: npmguard, ethui, Proof of Scan, Hardhat3-Ledger, BuildBlocks, Fill Me Up, TARE, Petri, Immunity.

### 4.2 What finalist judges reward (evidence)
- **A complete loop with an onchain artifact, shown live in 4 minutes.** Every 2026 finalist description names a concrete settlement step (USDC payout, NFT licence, ENS record, HCS message). Finalists must submit video + live demo + open code.
- **WOW via the senses.** Robot arm, silicon, NFC wristbands, AR glasses, SMS without internet, bank card recovery, handheld console. Hardware-based finalists took no sponsor prizes yet still placed — WOW alone can carry a finalist slot (EVM PORST, Lean Silicon, Carte Bleue, ETH Arcade, LeekWallet).
- **Legible technical depth.** Post-quantum signatures, FHE agents, ZK+TEE orchestration, self-arbitraging LPs, formally verified SwapVM — each pitched as one user sentence.
- **Timeliness.** Finalist slates mirror the quarter's narrative: EIP-7702 right after Pectra (BeamPay, Igris AI), x402 and ERC-8004 after their 2025–26 launches (Glassbox402, Wallet Shift, OnchainRouter), prompt-injection defence after agent exploits (ENShell, maki, Immunity), Portuguese housing in Lisbon, Thai parking in Bangkok, Argentine flight delays in BA.
- **Practicality ≠ users.** No finalist shows traction; the rubric asks "could the target audience use it today". Void Tactics and Novi Corpus show that a pre-existing product entered via Continuity can still reach the top-10 if the in-window delta is visible.
- **Polish is necessary, not sufficient.** No finalist is described by its UI; several (LYNX, Cumulant, TARE) are terminals/dashboards whose edge is the mechanism.

### 4.3 What sponsor-track judges reward (deep integration vs. token use)
- **Token use buys only pool money.** Blockscout: 81/217 teams ≈$122 each (Prague). ENS: 43 "Integrate ENS" pool awards of ≈$140 at NYC 2026 vs. $4k for the single "Best ENS Integration for AI Agents". World's 102 pool prizes in 2025 were replaced in 2026 by placed tracks.
- **Partners read code and count usage.** 0G repo-reviewed 100% of 67 submissions (85% "meaningful"); Arc published per-primitive adoption (40% Nanopayments) and winner dependence (67% "core infrastructure"); Uniswap and 0G demand feedback files and "what breaks without us" statements at Mumbai.
- **The flagship primitive must be load-bearing.** Winners build *on the sponsor's newest primitive*: SwapVM opcodes (Aqua0, Alba, Turing Swap), v4 hooks as the exchange (UniPerp, lpTOKEN.fun), World ID as pricing/eligibility (BookerBob, Proof-of-Human, equitas), ENS records as policy (Aegis, Kondor), CRE as gate (Canary, OpenPop, ENShell), Nanopayments as billing (VEIL, NanoCrawl, Onda), 0G Storage+Compute+iNFT together (0run).
- **Multi-layer use of one sponsor beats a single call.** 0run and Shawarma used three 0G layers; Mars used Arc's Agent Stack *and* settled verdicts on Hedera *and* gated auditors with World ID; Aegis combined ENS + CRE + Ledger. Thin four-sponsor compositions on HackMoney showcase pages (Velvet Arc: v4 + Yellow + LI.FI + ENS) did not place.
- **Sponsors reward their own narrative, which moves each quarter**: UF hooks → API/agents/privacy; 1inch Fusion+ → Aqua; World Mini Apps → AgentKit/World ID 4.0; ENS "best use" → agents/ENSv2; Chainlink CCIP → CRE/confidential; Hedera EVM → x402; Arc payments → Nanopayments/Agent Stack/Arc Studio.

### 4.4 Traits of projects that won BOTH finalist and multiple sponsor tracks
Documented cases: VEIL VPN (World 1st + Arc 1st + ENS 1st), DIVE (World 1st + Hedera + 0G 2nd), ArcBook (1inch 1st + Graph 2nd), KOLlateral (Uniswap 1st + 0G 3rd), Hands Unchained (World 1st + 0G 2nd), Immunity (Chainlink + ENS), Proof of Scan (Sui + ENS), Void Tactics (World + Dynamic), Aqua0 (World + 1inch + LayerZero), BMCP (Chainlink ×2 + Citrea), payload.exchange (Polygon + Coinbase + vlayer), conduct.chat (Flow + Hedera + Blockscout), DAOGenie (Flow + Hedera + Rome + Linea), Industry AI (Coinbase + Base + Scroll), PrivyCycle (Privy + Zircuit + 0G), Hashlocked (1inch 1st), Magnee (LI.FI + ENS). Shared traits:
1. **Remove any sponsor and the product breaks.** VEIL cannot bill without Nanopayments, cannot gate without World ID, cannot be discovered without ENS. ArcBook *is* an Aqua app; Hands Unchained *is* a World-ID labour market.
2. **The sponsors compose along one user flow**: identity (World) → payment/settlement (Arc, Hedera x402, 1inch Aqua) → data/verification/identity (ENS, The Graph, 0G, Chainlink CRE). Never three unrelated bolt-ons.
3. **Agent-economy framing** in 2026: 7 of the 8 multi-sponsor 2026 finalists are agent payments/verification/identity products.
4. **One sentence of user value + one of mechanism, repeated in README, video and pitch** — because partners never see the live pitch.
5. **A local/timely hook** (EthCC privacy, Devcon-week themes, city-specific pain).
6. **Money check**: a 3-person finalist team with one $3k sponsor placement (Proof of Scan, $7.6k) beat every pure stacker at NYC 2026; Mars ($6.75k) is the best non-finalist outcome. Finalist + two placed sponsor prizes is the ceiling; a third sponsor rarely adds more than ~$1.5–2.5k.

### 4.5 Anti-patterns (what consistently fails to place)
- **Ineligible by construction**: pre-event code in the Classic track; AI-generated submissions "without meaningful team contribution" (Mumbai rule); choosing continuity-only tracks (1inch top tier, Definitive, World [Cont]) from a from-scratch entry.
- **Horizontal, shallow SDK use** — the ENS/Blockscout pool pattern: dozens of $100 badges, zero placements, zero finalists.
- **Generic categories without a mechanism twist**: launchpads, portfolio dashboards, "chat with your DeFi", wallet-with-one-feature, generic DEX aggregators. The only tracker in a 2026 top-10 (Wallet Shift) indexed a brand-new standard (ERC-8004).
- **Agents that only talk.** 2026 finalists are agents that pay, are policed or are verified; unconstrained LLM-with-a-wallet demos appear in showcases by the hundred and never in finals.
- **Demos dependent on live third-party latency** (unseeded oracles, slow testnets) — winners pre-seed state and show the tx hash.
- **Protocol without UI, or UI without onchain settlement** — both fail Practicality; async partner judges additionally need a README that says *where in the code* their primitive lives.
- **Stacking four or five sponsors** — the corpus shows 0–8% of winners holding ≥3 sponsor prizes per event (NYC 6/99, Cannes 2/67, Lisbon 0/55); the third/fourth sponsor is usually a pool badge.

### 4.6 Tactical: stacking 3 partner programs at Mumbai
1. **Use the one-program-many-tracks rule.** Pick partners whose 2–3 tracks one build can hit: Hedera (x402 agent payments + tokenization + No-Solidity), Arkiv (mirror + from-scratch + Swarm bonus), Whitechain (stablecoin payments + RWA + gasless), Arc (five $3k Arc-Studio prizes), World (Agent Pass + IDKit). Three such partners = 6–9 shots on three selections.
2. **Target low-density, high-pool partners.** Fields seen: 0G 67 (Lisbon)/44 (Cannes) teams; Arc 69 (Cannes)/155 (HackMoney); Blockscout 81 pool winners; ENS 43 pool winners at NYC. Arkiv, Whitechain, Definitive and Shutter have **zero** ETHGlobal history and $15k/$5k pools; EF's $15k had no published criteria on 2026-11-02. Expect far fewer qualified entries there than at World/ENS/Uniswap.
3. **Design one user flow in which each primitive is load-bearing.** Stacks grounded in documented winners:
   - *Agent-commerce*: **Arc** (USDC/Nanopayments/escrow — VEIL, NanoCrawl, Clawback) + **World** (Agent Pass / IDKit gating which humans and agents may transact — Proof-of-Human, BookerBob) + **ENS** (agent identity/policy records — Aegis, Ghost in the Machine). Finalist angle: verifiable agent services with per-call billing and revocable authority.
   - *Verifiable-AI resolution*: **0G** (attested inference + encrypted memory — 0run, DIVE) + **Hedera** (x402 "AI Agents That Pay" + HCS evidence — Glassbox402, AgentGate, Mars) + **Chainlink** (CRE workflow that validates before a state change — Canary, OpenPop, ENShell). Finalist angle: prediction/parametric markets settled by attested agents (DIVE, Cumulant, Canary, AIMM).
   - *Data/infra public good*: **Arkiv** (mirror something Ethereum depends on) + **Ethereum Foundation** (Anon-RPC / alt-mempool privacy) + **Whitechain** (gasless stablecoin/RWA flow). Finalist angle: dev tooling with a physical or IRL demo (cf. Lean Silicon, ethui, TARE).
   - *Continuity DeFi*: pre-build the open-source base, enter Continuity, and take **1inch Aqua** (SwapVM program — RiverSwap, ArcBook, Solvent) + **Uniswap** (SwapVM/hook/API — Lotus, Sentinel, lpTOKEN.fun) + **Definitive** (Flash API/MCP orders). This is where ALMA/KOLlateral/Lotus-style mechanism work pays.
4. **Write for the async reader.** One README section per sponsor: primitive used, why removing it breaks the product, file paths, deployed addresses/tx hashes, a 30-second video timestamp; plus each partner's hygiene items (`FEEDBACK.md`, Arkiv schema/feedback files, 0G changelog + "what breaks" sentence, Definitive X post, 1inch git history, live x402 endpoint).
5. **Pitch order for the 4 minutes**: sensory WOW in minute one, onchain artifact by minute two, mechanism in minute three, sponsor map in the last 30 seconds — the order in which the rubric is scored.
6. **Expect a 25–40% chance of winning *something* at an in-person 2026 event, and ≈4% of a finalist slot** (10 of 173–265). The finalist slot is worth ≈$1,500 per teammate plus the Finalist Pack; two placed sponsor prizes add $3–6k. Plan for finalist + 2 placed sponsors; treat the third sponsor as optionality, not revenue.

## Executive summary (≈300 words)

Across 16 ETHGlobal events (Bangkok 2024 → ETHOnline 2026; 149 finalists, ~2,200 prize-winning projects) the record is consistent. **Two routes reach the top-10.** Route A is the sponsor-dense agent-economy build: 64% of finalists also won a sponsor prize and 27% won two or more, and in 2026 the multi-sponsor finalists (VEIL VPN, DIVE, ArcBook, KOLlateral, Hands Unchained) all made identity (World), payment (Arc Nanopayments, Hedera x402, 1inch Aqua) and data/identity (ENS, 0G, The Graph) primitives load-bearing along one user flow. Route B is sponsor-free deep tech with a physical WOW (EVM PORST, Lean Silicon, Carte Bleue, ETH Arcade, LeekWallet) — it earns the finalist slot but no sponsor money.

**Categories**: agent payments/security/verification rose from ≈20% of finalists in 2024–25 to ≈44% in 2026; prediction/parametric markets, privacy/ZK/PQ, locally-flavoured stablecoin payments, Uniswap-v4/Aqua mechanism design and dev/security tooling fill the rest; games have faded. Finalist judges score Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability and WOW in a 4-minute live demo and reward a complete onchain loop, legible depth, timeliness and a sensory demo. **Sponsor judges read code**: 0G verified "meaningful integration" in 85% of 67 repos; Arc reported 67% of winners depend on Nanopayments; Blockscout and ENS pay dozens of ≈$120–140 pool badges for token use while reserving $2–4k placements for builds that use their newest primitive as the mechanism (SwapVM opcodes, World ID as eligibility, ENS records as policy, CRE as gate).

**Anti-patterns**: pre-event code, AI-only builds, shallow multi-SDK use, chat-only agents, dashboards/launchpads, latency-dependent demos, and READMEs that hide where the integration lives. Three-plus sponsor stacks are rare (0–8% of winners).

**For Mumbai**: exploit "many tracks from one partner = one program" (Hedera, Arkiv, Whitechain, Arc, World); favour zero-history $15k sponsors (Arkiv, Whitechain, EF) where fields are thin; respect continuity-only tracks (1inch top tier, Definitive, World [Cont]); and build either an agent-commerce stack (Arc + World + ENS) or a verifiable-AI stack (0G + Hedera x402 + Chainlink CRE) in which removing any sponsor breaks the product. Budget for finalist + two placed sponsor prizes; the third is optionality.
